01 / Swiss market poster

Signal

A financial poster with a living liquidity field.

Brand-first / issuer teams
Explore Signal ↗
Concept, prompt & tradeoff +

What changes. Monumental typography, a narrow editorial rail and a full-width liquidity interruption. Strategy entries become oversized numbered lines.

Design prompt. Design a Swiss financial poster, not a software landing page. Interrupt monumental type with one meaningful liquidity field; carry the same strong rules and numbered rhythm through strategies and buyer stories.

Watch for. The strongest brand recall, but the long headline needs careful mobile line breaks.

Editorial lens: Brand challenger. No user-testing or conversion rank is implied.
02 / Precision range instrument

Caliper

The range instrument is the product signature.

Technical capital owners
Explore Caliper ↗
Concept, prompt & tradeoff +

What changes. A copy rail and a calibrated figure form one opening spread. Rulers, cross-references and open grid lines continue into every section.

Design prompt. Build a precision editorial instrument around two smooth liquidity envelopes. Put readable copy beside it, not on it. Use cobalt only for action and focus; maintain stable axes and explanatory labels.

Watch for. The mechanism is clearest here; the brand is quieter than the poster directions.

Editorial lens: Product-clarity pick. No user-testing or conversion rank is implied.
03 / Liquidity as a field

Contour

Capital has a shape. Give it a landscape.

Vision / design-led curators
Explore Contour ↗
Concept, prompt & tradeoff +

What changes. A panoramic field stretches beneath a compact opening. Large contour lines and a continuous reading axis replace conventional feature boxes.

Design prompt. Treat liquidity as a continuous price landscape. Use a wide shallow figure, cream type on deep forest and copper contours. Keep samples subordinate to smooth envelopes and explain intent versus execution.

Watch for. Atmospheric and original; the schematic label must prevent an impression of real portfolio data.

Editorial lens: Visual-identity pick. No user-testing or conversion rank is implied.
04 / The asset owner’s journal

Folio

A product page with the authority of a considered journal.

Foundations / reserve managers
Explore Folio ↗
Concept, prompt & tradeoff +

What changes. A restrained serif editorial lead, a numbered issue masthead and margin notes. Mechanism figures sit inside substantial reading spreads.

Design prompt. Create an asset owner’s journal with a Swiss grid, editorial serif leads, generous margins and concise figure captions. Explain the whole portfolio without turning the homepage into an academic paper.

Watch for. Trustworthy reading rhythm; less immediate software energy than the technical entries.

Editorial lens: Treasury conversation. No user-testing or conversion rank is implied.
05 / One platform, two mandates

Dual

Two institutional jobs make one product legible.

Issuers + treasury buyers
Explore Dual ↗
Concept, prompt & tradeoff +

What changes. One shared brand sits over two strongly divided buyer fields. A common mechanism reconnects them; selected focus changes context, not the product.

Design prompt. Organize the opening as two institutional mandates: build an asset market and operate reserve assets. Use one brand and one primary call, with a single underlying liquidity mechanism.

Watch for. Fast buyer recognition, at the cost of less first-screen engineering detail.

Editorial lens: Commercial clarity. No user-testing or conversion rank is implied.
06 / An architectural cutaway

Section

Separate decisions from ownership—and make the boundary beautiful.

Curators / integration teams
Explore Section ↗
Concept, prompt & tradeoff +

What changes. A cutaway diagram leads the right side; technical section lines and orange callouts explain strategy, vault and venue as different layers.

Design prompt. Draw a spare architectural cutaway of market inputs, strategy decisions, vault ownership and AMM execution. Never put signals in custody. Keep the first-screen proposition commercial, then reveal the system.

Watch for. The cleanest architecture story; commercial language must stay ahead of implementation detail.

Editorial lens: Architecture direction. No user-testing or conversion rank is implied.
07 / A controlled motion study

Sequence

One intentional transformation tells the product story.

A confident product launch
Explore Sequence ↗
Concept, prompt & tradeoff +

What changes. A centered typographic opening followed by a numbered Read / Shape / Run stage. Hard blue fields and stable alignments replace ambient animation.

Design prompt. Use ultramarine as a confident editorial field. Show one intentional Read–Shape–Run transition with a stable axis and clear stage labels. No looping display, animated profits or scroll traps.

Watch for. Distinctive and energetic; motion is optional and every stage must remain complete when paused.

Editorial lens: Product-energy pick. No user-testing or conversion rank is implied.
08 / The complete position

Ledger

A disciplined ledger makes complexity feel manageable.

Diligence / operating mandates
Explore Ledger ↗
Concept, prompt & tradeoff +

What changes. Horizontal rules, aligned definitions and a non-monetary position ledger. The whole page uses an accounting rhythm without fake balances.

Design prompt. Build an operating ledger with no invented money. Separate assets, liquidity, trading fees, rewards and costs. Use a bold editorial opening and aligned rows; preserve a calm burgundy-and-paper identity.

Watch for. The most accountable story; deliberately less expressive than Signal or Contour.

Editorial lens: Measurement direction. No user-testing or conversion rank is implied.
09 / A private research desk

Nocturne

Dark institutional restraint, without trading-terminal theatre.

Private capital / technical diligence
Explore Nocturne ↗
Concept, prompt & tradeoff +

What changes. A quiet amber locator, deep negative space, inset research figure and full-width strategy reading panels. No glowing cards or tickers.

Design prompt. Create a night research desk with charcoal, warm light and pale amber. Lead with huge calm typography, then a small precise figure. Avoid neon, tickers, gradients, glass and fake live market information.

Watch for. Memorable and private; careful contrast is essential for long-form reading.

Editorial lens: Dark-brand direction. No user-testing or conversion rank is implied.
10 / Shape the mandate together

Workshop

An invitation to shape a real liquidity mandate.

B2B discovery / curators
Explore Workshop ↗
Concept, prompt & tradeoff +

What changes. A structured mandate slip sits beside the proposition. Choosing a buyer focus highlights useful inputs, then the page returns to product mechanics.

Design prompt. Make an open professional workshop, not a lead-generation form. Pair a strong brand line with a small Assets / Market / Objective slip. Use disciplined green and a tangible schematic mechanism.

Watch for. Approachable and practical; the instrument keeps it from feeling like a consultancy questionnaire.

Editorial lens: Conversation-first pick. No user-testing or conversion rank is implied.
