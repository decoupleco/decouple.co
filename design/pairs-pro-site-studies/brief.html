Pairs.pro / Studio challenge 01

Ten identities.
Three voices.
One product.

Create ten original institutional homepages from the supplied wireframe. Hold product meaning steady while exploring radically different composition, rhythm and visual identity. Use three consistent copy decks so words can be compared independently from design.

The three copy decks

A / Always-on markets

Markets close. Liquidity never sleeps.
Lead with the tension between market hours and an always-on token market.. Hero, section leads, mechanism labels, CTA context and closing microcopy vary together. “Sharper AMM LP.” and “Book a call” remain stable brand/action anchors.

B / Asset-first liquidity

Liquidity shapes markets.
Help issuers recognize their asset before asking them to understand the architecture.. Hero, section leads, mechanism labels, CTA context and closing microcopy vary together. “Sharper AMM LP.” and “Book a call” remain stable brand/action anchors.

C / Engineering-led trust

One market. Three signals. A clear policy.
Make session-aware market design tangible through a single readable engineering drawing.. Hero, section leads, mechanism labels, CTA context and closing microcopy vary together. “Sharper AMM LP.” and “Book a call” remain stable brand/action anchors.

The common product story

Market information → strategy → liquidity position. Two smooth envelopes describe broad base and focused liquidity over fine tick samples. Samples are not individual positions. Profiles (Spot, Stable, LRT, Volatile) and strategy concepts are independent controls.

Use Optimal-Range, Follow-Tick, Vol+ and Market-Aware as the display names. Follow-Tick is a placement primitive; Aero-optimized operations are a venue-specific design, not guaranteed emission advantage. LRT uses a moving exchange-rate reference, not an assumed fixed peg.

Every study includes product, mechanism, four strategy families, the two institutional markets, private-vault design, venue context and a closing invitation. No return forecast, fake customer, TVL, APY, audit endorsement, wallet flow or deposit form.

The ten assignments

01 / Signal — Swiss market poster

Thesis. A financial poster with a living liquidity field.

Design a Swiss financial poster, not a software landing page. Interrupt monumental type with one meaningful liquidity field; carry the same strong rules and numbered rhythm through strategies and buyer stories.

Distinctive composition. Monumental typography, a narrow editorial rail and a full-width liquidity interruption. Strategy entries become oversized numbered lines.

Tradeoff to review. The strongest brand recall, but the long headline needs careful mobile line breaks.

Open Signal ↗

02 / Caliper — Precision range instrument

Thesis. The range instrument is the product signature.

Build a precision editorial instrument around two smooth liquidity envelopes. Put readable copy beside it, not on it. Use cobalt only for action and focus; maintain stable axes and explanatory labels.

Distinctive composition. A copy rail and a calibrated figure form one opening spread. Rulers, cross-references and open grid lines continue into every section.

Tradeoff to review. The mechanism is clearest here; the brand is quieter than the poster directions.

Open Caliper ↗

03 / Contour — Liquidity as a field

Thesis. Capital has a shape. Give it a landscape.

Treat liquidity as a continuous price landscape. Use a wide shallow figure, cream type on deep forest and copper contours. Keep samples subordinate to smooth envelopes and explain intent versus execution.

Distinctive composition. A panoramic field stretches beneath a compact opening. Large contour lines and a continuous reading axis replace conventional feature boxes.

Tradeoff to review. Atmospheric and original; the schematic label must prevent an impression of real portfolio data.

Open Contour ↗

04 / Folio — The asset owner’s journal

Thesis. A product page with the authority of a considered journal.

Create an asset owner’s journal with a Swiss grid, editorial serif leads, generous margins and concise figure captions. Explain the whole portfolio without turning the homepage into an academic paper.

Distinctive composition. A restrained serif editorial lead, a numbered issue masthead and margin notes. Mechanism figures sit inside substantial reading spreads.

Tradeoff to review. Trustworthy reading rhythm; less immediate software energy than the technical entries.

Open Folio ↗

05 / Dual — One platform, two mandates

Thesis. Two institutional jobs make one product legible.

Organize the opening as two institutional mandates: build an asset market and operate reserve assets. Use one brand and one primary call, with a single underlying liquidity mechanism.

Distinctive composition. One shared brand sits over two strongly divided buyer fields. A common mechanism reconnects them; selected focus changes context, not the product.

Tradeoff to review. Fast buyer recognition, at the cost of less first-screen engineering detail.

Open Dual ↗

06 / Section — An architectural cutaway

Thesis. Separate decisions from ownership—and make the boundary beautiful.

Draw a spare architectural cutaway of market inputs, strategy decisions, vault ownership and AMM execution. Never put signals in custody. Keep the first-screen proposition commercial, then reveal the system.

Distinctive composition. A cutaway diagram leads the right side; technical section lines and orange callouts explain strategy, vault and venue as different layers.

Tradeoff to review. The cleanest architecture story; commercial language must stay ahead of implementation detail.

Open Section ↗

07 / Sequence — A controlled motion study

Thesis. One intentional transformation tells the product story.

Use ultramarine as a confident editorial field. Show one intentional Read–Shape–Run transition with a stable axis and clear stage labels. No looping display, animated profits or scroll traps.

Distinctive composition. A centered typographic opening followed by a numbered Read / Shape / Run stage. Hard blue fields and stable alignments replace ambient animation.

Tradeoff to review. Distinctive and energetic; motion is optional and every stage must remain complete when paused.

Open Sequence ↗

08 / Ledger — The complete position

Thesis. A disciplined ledger makes complexity feel manageable.

Build an operating ledger with no invented money. Separate assets, liquidity, trading fees, rewards and costs. Use a bold editorial opening and aligned rows; preserve a calm burgundy-and-paper identity.

Distinctive composition. Horizontal rules, aligned definitions and a non-monetary position ledger. The whole page uses an accounting rhythm without fake balances.

Tradeoff to review. The most accountable story; deliberately less expressive than Signal or Contour.

Open Ledger ↗

09 / Nocturne — A private research desk

Thesis. Dark institutional restraint, without trading-terminal theatre.

Create a night research desk with charcoal, warm light and pale amber. Lead with huge calm typography, then a small precise figure. Avoid neon, tickers, gradients, glass and fake live market information.

Distinctive composition. A quiet amber locator, deep negative space, inset research figure and full-width strategy reading panels. No glowing cards or tickers.

Tradeoff to review. Memorable and private; careful contrast is essential for long-form reading.

Open Nocturne ↗

10 / Workshop — Shape the mandate together

Thesis. An invitation to shape a real liquidity mandate.

Make an open professional workshop, not a lead-generation form. Pair a strong brand line with a small Assets / Market / Objective slip. Use disciplined green and a tangible schematic mechanism.

Distinctive composition. A structured mandate slip sits beside the proposition. Choosing a buyer focus highlights useful inputs, then the page returns to product mechanics.

Tradeoff to review. Approachable and practical; the instrument keeps it from feeling like a consultancy questionnaire.

Open Workshop ↗

Review, not a popularity contest

These are ten single-author studies, not ten independent designers or user-tested winners. The gallery’s editorial lenses are hypotheses, not rankings. Compare the same copy deck and viewport. Write the strongest weakness before selecting a direction.

Interaction + accessibility

All controls are keyboard-operable. Profile and strategy choices update a labelled schematic. Book a call opens a local presentation with no data submission. Motion is intentional, optional and reduced-motion aware. Test at 1440 × 1000, 390 × 844 and 360 px; action targets are at least 44 px.

Reproduce

From the studio directory: node build.mjs, then node test.mjs. Serve dist/ with an ordinary local static server. The existing wireframe server can serve entrants/codex-studio/dist/index.html. No dependency install, remote fonts, runtime APIs or credentials.
