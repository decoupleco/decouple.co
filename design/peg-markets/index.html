peg.markets, in frames

The peg.markets app as a wireframe sheet: Swap, Markets and Channels for anyone and for a position holder, the Sharp Curve and Epoch Economics research pages, the curator's caps and the buffer's weekly settlement, and the states that keep it honest — drawn in the μi Sharp outline from the owner's PDF, the demo UI build and the demo specification. Every frame has an id the design brief cites (docs/DESIGN-CHALLENGE.md); the sheet prints to docs/refresh-wireframe.pdf.

Every figure comes from one fixture whose arithmetic is checked: the swap's 91.421774 pegEUR, the 1.90 health, the 7.6 bp fee from 24 % of the flow cap. Controls hold a static state. Charts read out on hover or with ← →. The quote is drawn in the series blue and the oracle as a dashed neutral, because orange would read as the caution colour.
