peg.markets refresh · 11 frames · round 2

peg.markets, in frames

The peg.markets app as a wireframe sheet, simplified to the owner's original flow — Swap, Markets, Channels and the wallet — and drawn in the live build's vocabulary: segmented tabs, grey inset amount panels with a token pill, one dark primary, details behind a disclosure. Every frame has an id the design brief cites (docs/DESIGN-CHALLENGE.md); the sheet prints to docs/refresh-wireframe.pdf.

Every figure comes from one fixture whose arithmetic is checked: the swap's 91.421774 pegEUR, the 1.90 health, the 7.6 bp fee from 24 % of the flow cap. Controls hold a static state. Charts read out on hover or with ← →. The quote is drawn in the series blue and the oracle as a dashed neutral, because orange would read as the caution colour.
