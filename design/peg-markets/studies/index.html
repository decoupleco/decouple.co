Currency markets, ten ways

Ten directions. One product.

Ten compositions of the same peg.markets journey — swap, markets, a market, mint, a channel, and two desktop workspaces — on the μi framework, with one fixture whose arithmetic is checked. Each direction is five structural choices (navigation, lead block, market row, chart, action placement) and a theme; every pair differs in at least three choices, so no direction is a recolour of another.

Shortlist (editorial, not user research): Weekly Flow for the product's own idea, Balance Sheet for a holder who thinks in positions, Index for the sharpest minimum. Study pages render without script; this gallery uses a few lines for filter and compare.
