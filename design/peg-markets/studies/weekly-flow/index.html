Weekly Flow

Thesis. The week is the unit of peg.markets: show where this week's flow stands against its cap, and every fee, lock and settlement explains itself.

  1. Hierarchy. The fee and its cause lead: every market and every swap opens on a signed flow gauge from −cap to +cap, with the fee it implies at the end. Balances come second.
  2. Row composition. A market is one gauge plus one physical-collateral bar (available | locked). No metric grid; borrower collateral and debt sit on a single quiet line, never added to LP funding.
  3. Navigation. A seven-cell week strip heads every screen (day 4 of 7, settles in 3 d), and the fourth tab is the Week itself: settlement is a place, not a hidden admin action.
  4. Action placement. The action docks above the tab bar; its consequences render as before → after pairs (LTV, health) instead of an "after" list.

Palette: μi Base, white surface, accent --ac for flow, --ok-b available and --wa-b locked collateral; quote in series blue, oracle as a dashed neutral. System fonts, tabular lining figures. Every figure comes from the shared fixture and closes under check.mjs. No script on this page.
