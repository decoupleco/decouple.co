The ten view blocks

The reusable views that index pools and lending markets, show one market, and manage a position — chosen over twenty review rounds against PegCore, Pairs Pro, Mezo Swap and Super Liquid. Each block has three to five variants, rendered live in your theme. The agent has marked a pick with its reason. Mark yours, say what to change, then Copy review and paste it back: the next round replaces the losing variants with ones built from your notes.

Markets table, Position card and Chart card follow the first human review; four agent review rounds (design language · information architecture · accessibility · budget and copy) have been applied since. Charts read out on hover or keyboard; the review text appears in a panel as well as on the clipboard.

Block 3 · PegCore · Pairs Pro · Mezo · Super Liquid

Position card

Risk is read from it. One information architecture for every position: identity, a bar that says how close to the edge, three rows (size · rate · accrued), the next action.

Constraint: The bar — health, range or term — always has three labels: low edge, where you are, high edge. "Accrued" means the same thing on every card: signed, + owed to you, − owed by you. Header labels stack vertically to keep the header short.
