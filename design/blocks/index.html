The ten view blocks

The reusable views that index pools and lending markets, show one market, and manage a position — chosen over twenty review rounds against PegCore, Pairs Pro, Mezo Swap and Super Liquid. Each block has three to five variants, rendered live in your theme. The agent has marked a pick with its reason. Mark yours, say what to change, then Copy review and paste it back: the next round replaces the losing variants with ones built from your notes.
